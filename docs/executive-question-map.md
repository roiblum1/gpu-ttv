# Executive question map (E01–E18)

The product contract of AGENT.md §13: each CEO question, the decision it supports, its release (§0.6), the pages and panels that answer it, its data dependencies, capability tier, status and acceptance test. Every panel's full definition (unit, population, window, filters, lineage, unknown-vs-zero behaviour, expected result) is in `config/panel-contracts/<page uid>.yaml`, whose schema is in `config/panel-contracts/README.md`. `tests/test_panel_contracts.py` keeps the contracts, the dashboards and this map in step.

**2026-11-25 ([ADR-0009](adr/0009-five-teams-and-manager-dashboards.md)).** The user replaced the ten pages with six "managers' flash board" pages: a summary (`ttv-home`) and one waste page per team (`ttv-samurai`, `ttv-redbull`, `ttv-popai`, `ttv-apply`, `ttv-ml`). They show GPUs and GPU-hours wasted, in plain words. On 2026-11-26 the user added the AWS On-Demand price of that waste (ADR-0003's present-day valuation, a comparison and not our bill) and the GPUs by model. The pages that answered E01, E02 and E18 in detail, and the missing-input cards for the R2/R3 questions, were deleted with them. Those questions now have no page. The ledger, the milestones and the engine's `/explain/{asset}` still hold the data, so a page can be added back when someone asks for it.

- **Status.** *built*: a page answers the question. *partial*: a page answers part of it. *no page*: no dashboard answers it since 2026-11-25; the "Data" column says what the ledger still holds. *missing input*: the inputs do not exist yet (§0.6).
- **Tier** (§13 capability tiers). *core*: infrastructure telemetry. *history*: retained ledger history. *business_input*: approved business or planning inputs (§4 "Optional business and planning inputs").
- **Panels** are `uid #id`. Each team page has the same seven panels: #1 GPUs wasted now, #2 GPU-hours wasted, #6 cost of waste at AWS prices, #3 why (reasons in plain words), #7 wasted GPUs by model, #4 trend by reason, #5 what is wasting (servers, models or jobs, with their GPU model and cost). Home: #1, #2, #11 cost of waste at AWS prices, #12 costing now, #5–#9 team tiles, #3 where every GPU is now, #4 data as of, #10 trend, #13 GPUs by model now.

## Keeping the map true

- A change to a panel updates its contract entry in the same commit (id, title, filters, query, expected result). `tests/test_panel_contracts.py` fails when they drift, when a contract answers a question whose row here does not name its page, or when a row names a page that no longer exists.
- `tests/test_dashboards_sql.py` executes every Postgres query on every page, with All and with one cluster selected, against a synthetic fixture, and checks the exact numbers.
